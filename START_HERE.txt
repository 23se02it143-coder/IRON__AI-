IRON-AI: how to open it (Windows)

1. Right-click the zip, Extract All, then open the extracted "iron-ai" folder.
2. Double-click  run.bat
   - First run: it installs everything (1 to 2 minutes, needs internet).
   - Your browser opens at http://localhost:8000 by itself.
   - The black window prints the ADMIN email and password. Copy them.
3. Keep the black window open while you use the site. Closing it stops the site.
   Press Ctrl+C in that window to stop it on purpose.

Admin panel: http://localhost:8000/admin

If the window shows an error, copy the last lines and send them to me.
If Python is missing, run.bat tells you how to install it (tick "Add python.exe to PATH").
The camera coach only works on http://localhost:8000 (not a LAN address) or on an HTTPS site.

Things to try

- "Start camera": live coaching. Stand side-on, whole body in frame, stand still for 2 seconds.
