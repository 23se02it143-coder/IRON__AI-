Who we are

{{ s.company_name }} operates {{ s.app_name }}. We are the controller of the personal data described here.{% if s.support_email %} Contact: {{ s.support_email }}.{% endif %}

What we collect, and why

  • Account: name, email and a password hash (we never store your password itself). Used to run your account.
  • Waitlist: email, optional name, interest and goal, plus the referral code and campaign tags of the link you came from. Used to manage launch access and referrals.
  • Contact messages: what you write to us, so we can reply.
  • Readiness assessment: your answers are sent to our server to be scored, and nothing is stored unless you choose to save your result. If you do, we keep your answers and the result so you can revisit them. Answers can relate to sleep, pain or injury, so we treat them as sensitive and store them only with your explicit consent.
  • Camera form coach: the pose tracking runs inside your browser, on your device. Your camera feed is never recorded, uploaded or stored. If you are signed in and finish a session, we save only its numbers: exercise, rep count, form scores, duration and the kinds of faults noticed, so you can see your progress.
  • Diet planner: the details you type (age, height, weight, goal) are sent to our server to calculate the plan and are not stored. If you press "Email me this plan" we use your email address to send it, and to add you to the waitlist only if you tick that box. Your last inputs are remembered in your own browser only.
  • Assistant chat: messages you send are processed to produce a reply and are not stored in your account.{% if s.assistant_uses_llm %} Replies may be generated by a third-party AI provider (Anthropic); the text of the conversation is sent to it for that purpose.{% endif %}
  • Billing: your plan, invoices and payment status. {% if s.payments_provider == 'stripe' %}Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers.{% elif s.payments_provider == 'razorpay' %}Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay and never touch our servers.{% else %}The service is in test mode and takes no real payments.{% endif %}
  • Usage counts: anonymous page and button counters. They store no IP address, cookie or account identifier, and reduce the referrer to its domain name.
  • Security logs: IP addresses are used briefly in memory to rate-limit abusive traffic.

Cookies

We set one strictly necessary cookie, iron_session, only when you sign in, to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers load on our pages.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We use service providers only to operate the service: email delivery{% if s.payments_provider == 'stripe' %}, payments (Stripe){% elif s.payments_provider == 'razorpay' %}, payments (Razorpay){% endif %}{% if s.assistant_uses_llm %}, and AI responses (Anthropic){% endif %}, plus hosting. They may process data only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

  • Account data until you delete your account.
  • Waitlist data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Invoices are kept without your personal details after account deletion, as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can see and change your name, download all your data as a file, and permanently delete your account from the account page. You can leave the waitlist through the link in any email. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to object, restrict processing or complain to your data-protection authority; contact us to use them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you before it takes effect.
